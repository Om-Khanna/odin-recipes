Lasagna Recipe

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, rich tomato meat sauce, creamy cheese, and melted mozzarella. It is warm, filling, and perfect for family dinners.

This recipe is simple to make and gives you crispy golden cheese on top with soft flavorful layers inside.

  • 9 lasagna sheets
  • 300g minced meat
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Boil the lasagna sheets until slightly soft.
  2. Cook onion and garlic in a little oil.
  3. Add minced meat and cook until browned.
  4. Add tomato sauce, salt, pepper, and oregano. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Spread a layer of sauce in a baking dish.
